## Recording so it lines up exactly

1. Start your camera, then start the video (or the teleprompter page) from **0:00.0**. Listen to the video in headphones so its sound doesn't get into your mic.
2. **Clap once exactly at 0:00.0.** The teleprompter counts 3, 2, 1 and flashes CLAP. That clap is your sync point.
3. Keep recording from the clap to your last line, even while others are speaking. Your take then sits on the video's timeline with no offset.
4. Start each line on its **In** time and finish before its **Out** time. If you finish early, stay silent. Don't stretch words or rush.
5. In the editor, line up the clap with frame 0 of the video, and keep every clip at **100% speed**.
6. Line 34 is said by all four together. Everyone should record it, so the editor can pick the cleanest take or layer them.

## The story in one breath

- **Problem:** after a public contract is signed, costs rise and new companies join quietly, and no one checks the proof. Officials lose track, and small firms can't find work that fits them.
- **Solution:** Auditly spots every change, looks for the proof, scores which contracts to check first, shows hidden links between people and companies, and matches small firms to jobs they can win.
